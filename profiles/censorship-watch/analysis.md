# Evaluation goal

Evaluate whether the item provides a useful, timely signal about internet censorship or access disruption while resisting the urge to turn a rumour into a confirmed blocking event.

# Scoring rubric

- **10:** Reproducible new blocking mechanism, critical nationwide event, or major protocol disruption supported by measurements.
- **9:** Confirmed blocking across several independent networks or a strong reproducible research result.
- **8:** Significant measured restriction, shutdown, or well-supported change in censorship technique.
- **7:** Well-documented field incident with network, place, time, affected traffic, and more than one source.
- **6:** Technically detailed observation from one operator or a useful preliminary experiment.
- **4-5:** Unconfirmed observation with some concrete details.
- **2-3:** Repetition, policy-only story, or one user's problem without diagnostic evidence.
- **0-1:** Advertising, vague claim, affiliate content, or unrelated political commentary.

# Hard calibration rules

A single field report cannot score above 5.9. A Russian event receives no automatic credibility boost; add importance only after the evidence threshold is met. A general outage must not be labelled as targeted VPN blocking. Treat OONI anomalies as signals rather than proof unless the measurement and methodology support the claimed mechanism. Use three to five tags, including country, access type, and mechanism when known.
