# Censorship watch profile

Use this profile for timely evidence about internet censorship, VPN or protocol blocking, throttling, connection resets, active probing, allowlists, app or repository distribution restrictions, and regional or national internet shutdowns.

Include reproducible measurements, research reports, operator- or ASN-specific observations, technically detailed field reports, official project confirmations, and credible reports that distinguish mobile, fixed, regional, and national effects.

Do not use this profile for political news without a change in network access, repeated reporting derived from one source, a single unexplained "it does not work" message, generic VPN marketing, or claims that omit place, network, time, affected protocol, and observable behaviour.
