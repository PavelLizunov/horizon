# Evaluation goal

Evaluate whether this is an important, concrete engineering development for people who build, operate, audit, or depend on VPN and censorship-circumvention systems.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10:** Critical vulnerability or supply-chain incident; new major protocol or transport; mass breakage; major, verified change in detection or blocking resistance.
- **8:** Major stable release, breaking migration, new masking mechanism, or significant platform/distribution change.
- **7:** Useful upstream change with clear operational impact and concrete technical details.
- **6:** Narrow but substantive compatibility change or credible experimental project.
- **4-5:** Small release, preliminary experiment, or weakly documented observation.
- **2-3:** Routine bug fix, tutorial, configuration recipe, or repeated known information.
- **0-1:** Advertising, discount, ranking, affiliate content, vague outage report, or unsupported marketing claim.

# Evidence discipline

Prefer upstream release notes, maintainers, reproducible research, advisories, commits, and documented compatibility reports. A vendor announcement proves what the vendor announced, not that performance or evasion claims are independently true. Do not reward hype or popularity. Use three to five specific tags, including the affected project or protocol when known.
