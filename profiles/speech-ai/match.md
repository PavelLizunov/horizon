# Speech & audio AI profile

Use this profile for substantive advancements in neural text-to-speech (TTS), automatic speech recognition (ASR), speaker diarization, voice cloning, audio separation, speech normalization, and local video dubbing pipelines.

Typical items include:
- Open-weights TTS models and engines: TeraTTSv2 (ONNX Runtime), Fish Audio / Fish-Speech (S2 Pro Dual-AR), Qwen3-TTS, Kokoro, F5-TTS, CosyVoice, StyleTTS2, Silero.
- Streaming and batch ASR architectures: Microsoft VibeVoice ASR, OpenAI Whisper optimizations (faster-whisper, mlx-whisper, whisper.cpp), Sber GigaAM v2/v3, and streaming word-level timestamp alignment.
- Audio diarization and source separation: Pyannote.audio, NeMo TitaNet, 3D-Speaker embeddings, Demucs (htdemucs), Mel-Band Roformer.
- Speech front-end engineering: CMUDict pronunciation modeling, IT-acronym transliteration, stress mark prediction (`ruaccent`), text normalization for mathematical expressions and numbers.
- Automated dubbing and meeting pipelines: `translate-fit` duration fitting, WSOLA/rubberband time-stretching, dynamic audio ducking, EBU R128 loudness normalization, and low-latency Web Audio/WASAPI/CoreAudio streaming.

Do not use this profile for generic music generation novelty apps, commercial consumer voice changers, marketing announcements of audiobooks without technical model details, or low-quality synthetic audio demos.
