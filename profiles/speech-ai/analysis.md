# Evaluation goal

Evaluate whether the item provides a substantive technical advancement in speech synthesis (TTS), recognition (ASR), voice cloning, diarization, or speech normalization with open weights or reproducible engineering utility.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Major milestone.** Paradigm-shifting open speech model (e.g. Fish-Speech S2 Pro release, VibeVoice streaming ASR, next-gen Whisper, GigaAM v3), novel low-latency architecture with real-time factor (RTF < 0.1), or breakthrough zero-shot voice cloning.
- **7-8: High engineering value.** Substantial open-weights TTS/ASR model releases, ONNX Runtime/TensorRT optimizations for speech, verified diarization improvements (Pyannote 3.5+), robust multilingual speech front-end tools, or production dubbing pipeline architectures.
- **5-6: Useful tool/model.** Incremental update to existing speech repositories, fine-tuned community checkpoints, or useful benchmarks comparing speech engines.
- **3-4: Minor update.** Small bug fix, minor voice dataset release, or basic tutorial on standard speech APIs.
- **0-2: Noise.** Generic AI music generators, promotional voice-clone marketing, low-effort speech clips without technical documentation.
