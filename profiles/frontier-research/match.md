# Frontier AI research & reasoning profile

Use this profile for substantive machine learning and artificial intelligence research papers, foundational model architectures, inference-time reasoning breakthroughs, post-training paradigms, and rigorous mathematical/empirical findings.

Typical items include:
- Test-time compute scaling, tree-search reasoning, self-correction, Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS) variants, and verification loops.
- Algorithmic innovations in reinforcement learning: Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO), verifiable reward functions (code execution, formal math proofs), self-play, and reward model distillation.
- Novel neural architectures: Multi-Head Latent Attention (MLA), sparse fine-grained Mixture of Experts (MoE), linear attention, state-space models (Mamba 2, RWKV, DeltaNet), and hybrid Transformer-SSM designs.
- Synthetic data generation, rejection sampling, and distillation of reasoning capabilities into smaller dense networks (1.5B–14B).
- Native end-to-end multimodal reasoning (speech-to-speech reasoning without ASR/TTS bridges, physical world modeling).
- Peer-reviewed research, top preprint releases (arXiv cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.CL), and technical reports from premier AI labs (DeepSeek, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Meta FAIR).

Do not use this profile for incremental hyperparameter tuning papers, unevidenced preprint claims without code or ablation studies, corporate re-announcements of known research, or AI philosophy/ethics essays without empirical data.
