# Evaluation goal

Evaluate whether the paper or technical report introduces a durable, reproducible scientific or algorithmic contribution to artificial intelligence, reasoning, or neural network architecture.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Paradigm-defining breakthrough.** Introduces a seminal algorithm or architecture that changes how models are trained or reasoned with (e.g. GRPO, MLA, Transformer, test-time compute scaling law paper), validated by rigorous ablation and public weights/reproducibility.
- **7-8: High scientific value.** Substantial architectural improvement, novel RL/post-training method with verified benchmark gains on hard evaluations (AIME, GPQA Diamond, SWE-bench, FrontierMath), or clean mathematical insight into reasoning mechanisms.
- **5-6: Competent research.** Solid empirical investigation, interesting application of known techniques, or useful ablation study with moderate novelty.
- **3-4: Marginal value.** Minor incremental tweak on benchmark leaderboards, lacking thorough ablations, or narrow domain utility without broader applicability.
- **0-2: Noise / Speculative.** Preprints making wild unverified claims without code or baseline comparisons, promotional academic hype.
