# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the importance of video content (transcripts and visual summaries) for readers interested in software engineering, artificial intelligence, tools, homelab/self-hosting, and the technology industry.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Groundbreaking.** First looks at major releases, exclusive demonstrations, paradigm-shifting explanations.
- **7-8: High value.** Substantive technical content: real demos, deep analysis, novel tools or workflows, important news discussion with concrete details.
- **5-6: Interesting.** Useful commentary, overviews, tutorials of moderate novelty, opinion pieces with substantive claims.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Routine commentary, rehash of widely known news, shallow treatment.
- **0-2: Noise.** Clickbait without substance, pure promotion, livestream placeholders.

# Evaluation guidance

Judge the informational value of the underlying content, not the production format. Do NOT penalize informal, conversational transcript style or missing polish: spoken transcripts are naturally loose. Extract the actual claims, tools, numbers and events mentioned, and score on those. On-screen text captured in visual summaries counts as concrete detail. Do not reward exaggerated titles.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
