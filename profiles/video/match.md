# Video profile

Use this profile for YouTube videos from monitored channels. The item content is the video's subtitle transcript and/or an AI visual summary built from storyboard frames, plus the author's description.

Typical items include tech commentary, release/tool overviews, tutorials, demos, homelab/self-hosting guides, and AI news discussion presented as video.

Do not use this profile for livestream announcements without substance, pure vlogs without informational content, or promotional material without substantive information.
